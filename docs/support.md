# PeptideX Support

We're a small team and we read every message.

## Contact

- **Email:** support@peptidesai.com
- **Response time:** within 2 business days

When reaching out, please include:

1. Your device model and iOS version
2. PeptideX version (Settings → About)
3. A short description of what you expected and what actually happened
4. Steps to reproduce, if the issue is repeatable

## Common questions

### How do I export my data?
PeptideX Pro subscribers can export from **Profile → Data Export**, choosing
CSV (for spreadsheets), JSON (full backup including protocols and entries),
or PDF (printable report). If you are not a Pro subscriber and need a copy
of your data for legal or portability reasons, email privacy@peptidesai.com
and we will help at no charge.

### How do I delete all my data?
Uninstall the app. All local data is removed with it. If you signed in with
Apple, also revoke access in **Settings → Apple Account → Sign in with Apple →
PeptideX**.

### Does PeptideX work without signing in?
Yes. Every feature works without an account. Sign in with Apple is optional
and is only used to personalize the app.

### Can I use PeptideX without granting HealthKit access?
Yes. HealthKit correlation is optional. Denying access does not disable any
other feature.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: **Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions**.
From there you can cancel or change plans. Your purchase remains active until
the end of the current billing period.

### I bought Pro on another device — why don't I see it here?
Open the paywall in PeptideX and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're
signed into the App Store with the same Apple ID used for the original
purchase.

### Something's wrong with a dose reminder
Check **Settings → Notifications → PeptideX** and confirm notifications are
allowed. iOS caps scheduled local notifications at 64; PeptideX consolidates
reminders per time slot to stay under that limit.
