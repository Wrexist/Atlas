PeptideX

Native iOS educational reference and self-tracking journal for people who already follow peptide supplementation protocols on the advice of a qualified clinician. Private by default, built with Swift and SwiftUI, powered entirely on-device.

PeptideX does not prescribe, recommend, or calculate doses. Every value is labeled as reported in the published research literature with citations on each peptide page.

Included for every user (free)

  • Peptide database: 208 peptides across 6 categories with research citations
  • Protocol builder: create, schedule, pause, and complete up to 3 active protocols
  • Dose logging: daily logging with injection site, dose, time, and notes
  • Local dose reminders: actionable on-device notifications with Mark as Taken / Snooze
  • Basic compliance analytics: streaks, weekly heatmap, day-of-week patterns
  • Read-only Apple Health integration: heart rate, HRV, sleep, activity
  • Compliance widget: today's compliance ring on your Home Screen
  • 14 achievements across dose milestones, streaks, and logging consistency
  • Custom peptides: add your own entries to the library
  • No analytics, no tracking, no proprietary backend

Medical disclaimer

PeptideX is an educational reference and self-tracking journal. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, dose recommendations, or dose calculations. Information about peptides is summarized from published research, with citations visible on each peptide page. Many substances referenced are research chemicals not approved for human use in many jurisdictions. Always consult a qualified, licensed healthcare provider before starting, changing, or stopping any protocol.
