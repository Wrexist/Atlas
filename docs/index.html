PeptideX

Native iOS app for tracking peptide supplementation protocols — private by default, built with Swift and SwiftUI, powered entirely on-device.

Features

  • Peptide database: 208 peptides across 6 categories, with research links
  • Protocol builder: create, schedule, pause, and complete protocols
  • Dose tracking: daily logging with injection site, actual dose, and notes
  • Compliance analytics: streaks, heatmaps, trends (Swift Charts)
  • HealthKit correlation: read-only heart rate, HRV, body mass, activity
  • Widgets: next dose and compliance ring on your Home Screen
  • Apple Watch companion: next-dose glance on your wrist
  • Notifications: actionable local dose reminders
  • Export: CSV, JSON, or PDF backup anytime (Pro)
  • iCloud sync: across your devices via your private iCloud database
  • No analytics, no tracking, no proprietary backend
