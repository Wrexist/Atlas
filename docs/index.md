# PeptideX

Native iOS app for tracking peptide supplementation protocols — private by
default, built with Swift and SwiftUI, powered entirely on-device.

- **Peptide database:** 208 peptides across 6 categories, with research links
- **Protocol builder:** create, schedule, pause, and complete protocols
- **Dose tracking:** daily logging with injection site, actual dose, and notes
- **Compliance analytics:** streaks, heatmaps, trends (Swift Charts)
- **HealthKit correlation:** read-only sleep, HRV, heart rate, activity
- **Widgets:** next dose and compliance ring on your Home Screen
- **Notifications:** actionable local dose reminders
- **Export:** CSV or JSON backup anytime
- **No backend, no analytics, no tracking**
