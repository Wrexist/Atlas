# PeptideX — Premium App Icon Prompts v2

> Designed marks with restrained depth — NOT 3D glass renders.
>
> These prompts prioritize **iconic readability at small sizes**, bold silhouettes,
> and the confidence of luxury brand marks. Liquid glass is used as a subtle material
> hint, not the entire concept.
>
> Paste any prompt below into Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Ideogram, or Flux.
> All prompts target **1024 x 1024**, centered, App Store ready.

## 1. The Embossed Mark

```
A premium iOS app icon, 1024x1024. Pure matte black background. In the center, a single abstract peptide symbol: three clean geometric circles connected in a tight triangular formation by thin straight lines, like a minimal molecular diagram reduced to its absolute essence. The symbol is rendered as a subtle embossed relief — not fully 3D, just gently raised from the surface with a fine emerald-green edge light (#6BAF7E) catching the upper-left edges and a barely-visible shadow on the lower-right. The surface has a soft matte-to-satin finish like anodized black aluminum. No glass bubbles, no transparency, no fog, no reflective floor. Feels like a luxury brand mark stamped into dark metal. Flat composition, overhead camera angle, single directional light, product design render, ultra-minimal, 8K.
```

**Why it works:** Reads at any size. The embossed relief gives dimension without screaming "3D render." Feels like something stamped on premium packaging.

**Why it works:** Dual-read: it's a "P" and a helix at once. Custom lettermarks are the gold standard for premium app icons (Notion, Linear, Arc). The glow adds just enough iOS 26 energy without going full glass.

## Usage Tips

### Generation Settings
- **Midjourney v6.1+:** Append `--ar 1:1 --s 250 --style raw` (lower stylize = less AI "flavor"). Negative: `--no glass bubbles, fog, mist, teal, water, reflective floor, gradient background`
- **DALL-E 3:** Prepend "I NEED this exact design, do not add extra elements." to fight DALL-E's tendency to embellish.
- **Ideogram 2.0:** Select "Design" style (not "3D Render"). Aspect 1:1.
- **Flux Pro:** These prompts work directly. Use `--guidance 3.5` for tighter prompt adherence.

### Post-Generation Checklist
1. Apply iOS squircle mask and check at **60x60**, **120x120**, **180x180** px — does the mark still read?
2. Check the green lands near `#4A7C59` or `#6BAF7E` — not teal, not lime, not neon
3. Verify the background is true black, not dark gray or dark teal (AI generators love to drift)
4. If the AI added fog, reflective surfaces, or glass bubbles you didn't ask for — regenerate or paint over in post
5. The icon should look confident sitting next to Apple Health, Oura, and Whoop on a home screen
